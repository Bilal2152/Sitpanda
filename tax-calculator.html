How Progressive Tax Works

Your income is divided into slices (brackets). Each slice is taxed at its own rate. Moving into a higher bracket does not mean all your income is taxed at that higher rate—only the income above the threshold is.

Effective vs Marginal Rate

  • Marginal Rate: The rate applied to your last dollar of income.
  • Effective Rate: Your total tax divided by your gross income.
