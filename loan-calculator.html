How to Use This Loan Calculator

This free loan calculator works for personal loans, auto loans, student loans, and business loans in Pakistan (PKR), India (INR), the United States (USD), the United Kingdom (GBP), the UAE (AED), and Saudi Arabia (SAR). Select your loan type, enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, and term, and the calculator instantly shows your monthly EMI.

For auto loans, you can enter a down payment to reduce the amount you borrow. For all loan types, you can add an extra monthly payment to see how much interest you save and how many months you cut from your loan.

Loan EMI Formula Used

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

  • P = Loan amount (principal)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Number of monthly instalments (years × 12)

Country-Specific Loan Notes

  • Pakistan: Bank personal loans are typically priced at KIBOR + bank spread. As of late 2026, 1-year KIBOR is around 12.2–12.7%, with personal loan rates often in the 15–20% range. Auto loans usually require a down payment and may use floating or fixed rates. Islamic financing (Murabaha/Ijarah) is also widely available and uses different profit-rate structures.
  • India: Personal loan rates typically range from 10.5% to 24%, with average rates around 13.5%. Auto loans range from 7.45% to 12.5%, and education loans can start as low as 7.25% for domestic courses. Rates depend heavily on credit score and lender.
  • United States: Personal loan APRs typically range from 7% to 36%, with average rates around 12%. New auto loans average around 6.8–7.5%, and federal student loans are around 3–6.5% depending on the loan type. Private student loans and auto loans vary widely by credit score.
  • United Kingdom: Personal loan APRs typically range from 5% to 15% (average around 8.9%). Car finance (HP/PCP) typically ranges from 5% to 12%. Student loan repayment is income-based and not a standard amortizing loan — the calculator provides an estimate only.
  • UAE & Saudi Arabia: Personal loan rates typically range from 4% to 12% (reducing balance), with auto loan rates often lower at 3% to 8% for new cars. Saudi Arabia's SAMA caps debt burden at 33% of income and allows terms up to 60 months for personal loans. Islamic finance options are common across the region.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this loan calculator free? Yes. All SitPanda calculators are 100% free with no signup required.

Does it work for Pakistan and India? Yes. The calculator supports PKR and INR, and defaults adjust based on your detected country. You can also change the currency and country manually.

What is the difference between EMI and total cost? EMI is your fixed monthly payment (principal + interest). Total cost is the sum of all EMI payments over the loan term — it includes both the principal you borrowed and the total interest you pay.

How does extra payment help? Any extra amount you pay each month goes directly toward the principal. This reduces your outstanding balance faster, which means less interest accrues over time and your loan is paid off sooner.

Are student loans calculated the same way? In many countries, student loans follow standard amortization and can be estimated with this tool. However, some countries (like the UK) use income-based repayment, and some US federal loans have special forgiveness programs. Always check your loan agreement for exact terms.
