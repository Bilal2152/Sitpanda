Loan EMI Formula Used

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula used by banks worldwide:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

  • P = Loan amount (principal)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Number of monthly instalments (years × 12)

Types of Loans

  • Personal loans — unsecured borrowing for any purpose. Rates depend on credit score and are usually higher than secured loans.
  • Auto loans — secured by the vehicle. Usually require a down payment (10–20%) and offer lower rates than personal loans.
  • Student loans — for education expenses. Often have subsidized rates or income-based repayment options. Terms and rules vary widely by country.
  • Business loans — for business expenses. Rates depend on business credit, revenue, and collateral.

Fixed vs Variable Interest Rates

Fixed rates stay the same for the entire loan term — predictable payments. Variable rates move with market benchmarks (like SOFR, EURIBOR, KIBOR, or a central bank base rate), so payments can rise or fall. Many loans offer a fixed initial period followed by a variable rate.

Frequently Asked Questions (Global)

Is this loan calculator free? Yes. All SitPanda calculators are 100% free with no signup required.

Does it work for any country? Yes. Select your country to load typical local rates and terms, or enter your own numbers. The calculator supports all major currencies and formats amounts according to your locale.

What is EMI? EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed amount you pay each month to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest.

What is the difference between EMI and total cost? EMI is your fixed monthly payment. Total cost is the sum of all EMI payments over the loan term — it includes the principal you borrowed plus the total interest you pay.

How does extra payment help? Any extra amount you pay each month goes directly toward the principal. This reduces your outstanding balance faster, which means less interest accrues over time and your loan is paid off sooner.

Are student loans calculated the same way? In many countries, student loans follow standard amortization and can be estimated with this tool. However, some countries use income-based repayment or special forgiveness programs — always check your loan agreement for exact terms.

Can I pay off my loan early? Many lenders allow early repayment, but some charge prepayment penalties. Check your loan agreement — early repayment can save significant interest.

What credit score do I need? Credit score requirements vary by country, lender, and loan type. Generally, higher credit scores unlock lower interest rates and better terms. Check with your specific lender for exact requirements.
