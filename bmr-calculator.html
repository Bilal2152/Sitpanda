What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body needs to accomplish its most basic (basal) life-sustaining functions, such as breathing, circulation, nutrient processing, and cell production. It accounts for about 60-70% of your total daily calorie expenditure.

BMR vs TDEE: What's the Difference?

While BMR is the calories you burn at complete rest, TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is the total calories you burn when you factor in daily activities (walking, working, exercising). To calculate your TDEE, you multiply your BMR by an activity factor (which is exactly what the table on this page shows).

Why Does BMR Matter?

  • It helps you understand how many calories your body naturally burns.
  • If you want to lose weight, you need to eat less than your TDEE (often calculated as BMR × Activity Factor).
  • If you want to gain muscle, you need to eat more than your TDEE.
