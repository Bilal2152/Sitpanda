How to Use This Calculator

Enter your gender, age, height, weight, and activity level. The calculator will determine your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure), which is the number of calories you burn daily. To lose weight, eat 500 calories less than your TDEE. To gain weight, eat 500 calories more.

What are Macros?

Macronutrients are the three main components of your diet: Protein, Carbohydrates, and Fats. This calculator uses a balanced 30/40/30 split, which is widely recommended for general health and fitness.
