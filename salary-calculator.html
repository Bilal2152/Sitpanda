How to Use This Salary Calculator

This free salary calculator converts between hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, and annual pay in any country and currency. Choose your pay period, enter the amount, and adjust hours per week and weeks per year to match your actual work situation.

The tool instantly shows your gross pay across all periods. Use the tax slider for a rough net (take-home) estimate — but remember that real tax rules vary widely by country, income level, and personal deductions.

Salary Conversion Formulas Used

  • Annual (from hourly): Hourly × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year
  • Monthly: Annual ÷ 12
  • Weekly: Annual ÷ 52
  • Daily (from hourly): Hourly × (Hours per Week ÷ 5) — assumes a 5-day week
  • Hourly (from annual): Annual ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

These are standard gross-pay conversions. They do not include overtime, bonuses, or unpaid leave adjustments unless you manually change the weeks-per-year value.

Gross Pay vs Net Pay — What’s the Difference?

Gross pay is your earnings before any deductions. Net pay (take-home pay) is what remains after income tax, social security contributions, pension contributions, insurance, and other deductions.

  • Gross conversions are universal — the same formulas work in every country.
  • Net pay is country-specific. Progressive tax bands, tax-free allowances, and social contribution rates differ significantly worldwide.

The tax slider on this page gives a rough global estimate. For precise net pay, use a country-specific payroll calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions (Global)

Is this salary calculator free? Yes. All SitPanda calculators are 100% free with no signup required.

Does it work for any country? Yes. Select your country to load typical working-hour defaults, or enter your own hours and weeks. The calculator supports all major currencies and formats amounts according to your locale.

How do I convert my hourly wage to an annual salary? Multiply your hourly rate by the number of hours you work per week, then by the number of weeks you are paid for in a year. For a standard full-time schedule (40 hours × 52 weeks), a $25/hour wage equals $52,000 per year.

Why is a month not exactly 4 weeks? A year has 52 weeks, not 48. So a monthly salary is annual ÷ 12, which is slightly more than 4 weekly paychecks. For example, $1,000/week is $4,333/month, not $4,000.

What if I have unpaid leave? Reduce the “Weeks per Year” value. For example, 2 unpaid weeks means using 50 instead of 52. The calculator will adjust your annual and monthly figures accordingly.

Does this include overtime or bonuses? No. This tool converts base pay between periods. Overtime, bonuses, and commissions are separate and should be added manually if needed.

How accurate is the net pay estimate? The slider uses a single flat tax rate for simplicity. Real net pay depends on progressive tax brackets, social security, pension, health insurance, and personal deductions — all of which vary by country. Treat the net figure as a rough planning estimate only.

Can I compare salaries across countries? Yes. Switch the country and currency dropdowns to see the same nominal amount in different currencies and with different working-hour assumptions. Note that this is a nominal comparison — it does not adjust for purchasing power or cost of living.
