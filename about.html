About SitPanda

Free, fast, and accurate online calculators for everyone.

Our Mission

Welcome to SitPanda! Our mission is simple: to provide the world with free, accessible, and highly accurate online calculators that make everyday life a little bit easier.

Whether you are planning a home loan, tracking your fitness goals, calculating your salary, or just trying to figure out a percentage, we believe you shouldn't have to sign up, pay a fee, or navigate through endless clutter to get the answer you need.

Why Trust SitPanda?

  • Verified Formulas: We don't guess. All of our financial, health, and mathematical calculators use standard, globally recognized formulas and methodologies.
  • Privacy First: All calculations happen instantly in your browser. We don't store your financial data, health metrics, or personal inputs.
  • Global Reach: Our tools are built for a global audience, supporting multiple currencies, measurement units, and country-specific defaults.
  • No Signups: We believe in instant utility. No accounts, no emails, no paywalls.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Bilal, the creator of SitPanda. I built this platform out of frustration with existing calculator websites that were slow, bloated with ads, or required personal information just to do basic math.

I'm passionate about clean design, data accuracy, and building tools that respect the user's time and privacy. SitPanda is a continuously growing project, and I am constantly working to add new tools and improve the existing ones based on user feedback.
