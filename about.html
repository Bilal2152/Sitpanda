About SitPanda

Calculate Anything. Instantly.

SitPanda was born out of a simple frustration: finding a fast, reliable, and ad-free calculator online was surprisingly difficult. Most websites were cluttered with pop-ups, required sign-ups, or were frustratingly slow to load.

We believe that utility tools should be just that—utilitarian. That's why we built SitPanda to be blazing fast. Our calculators run entirely in your browser, meaning results update instantly as you type, with zero waiting time.

Our Core Principles

1. Privacy by Design: We don't store the numbers you enter. All calculations are done locally on your device.

2. No Sign-ups Required: Every tool on SitPanda is 100% free to use. No accounts, no paywalls, no hidden fees.

3. Accuracy: Whether it's a complex mortgage amortization schedule or a simple BMI check, we use industry-standard formulas and verify them carefully.

4. Built for Mobile: We know most people use calculators on their phones. Our interface is touch-friendly and responsive, ensuring you never have to zoom in to read a result.

What's Next?

We are constantly adding new calculators based on user requests. If there's a tool you need that we don't have yet, please reach out via our Contact page.
