Mortgage Formula Used

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula used by lenders worldwide:

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

  • M = Monthly payment (principal + interest)
  • P = Loan amount (home price − down payment)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Number of monthly payments (years × 12)

What's Included in a Monthly Mortgage Payment?

A typical monthly mortgage payment includes principal (repaying the amount you borrowed) and interest (the cost of borrowing). In many countries, lenders also collect additional amounts for:

  • Property tax — usually calculated as a percentage of the home's assessed value
  • Home insurance — protects against fire, theft, and natural disasters
  • Mortgage insurance — required in some countries when the down payment is below a certain threshold (known as PMI in the US, CMHC insurance in Canada, LMI in Australia)

Fixed vs Variable Interest Rates

Fixed-rate mortgages lock in your interest rate for the entire term, giving predictable payments. Variable-rate mortgages move with market benchmarks (like KIBOR, SOFR, EURIBOR, or a central bank base rate), so payments can rise or fall. Many countries offer hybrid products that are fixed for an initial period and variable afterward.

Frequently Asked Questions (Global)

Is this mortgage calculator free? Yes. All SitPanda calculators are 100% free with no signup required.

Does it work for any country? Yes. Select your country from the dropdown to load typical local rates and loan terms, or enter your own numbers. The calculator supports all major currencies and formats amounts according to your locale.

What is the difference between principal & interest and total monthly cost? Principal and interest is the core loan repayment. The total monthly cost adds property tax, home insurance, and mortgage insurance (if applicable), giving a more complete picture of what you'll pay each month.

How much down payment do I need? Down payment requirements vary widely by country and lender — commonly 5–20% of the home price. A larger down payment reduces your loan amount, monthly payment, and total interest, and may help you avoid mortgage insurance.

How does the loan term affect my payment? A longer term lowers your monthly payment but increases total interest paid. A shorter term raises the monthly payment but saves interest over the life of the loan.

Can I pay off my mortgage early? Many lenders allow early repayment, but some charge prepayment penalties. Check your loan agreement — early repayment can save significant interest.

What is amortization? Amortization is the process of paying off a loan over time. In the early years, most of your payment goes to interest; in later years, more goes to principal. The amortization schedule shows this breakdown month by month.
