How to Use This Mortgage Calculator

This free mortgage calculator works for home loans in Pakistan (PKR), India (INR), the United States (USD), the United Kingdom (GBP), the UAE (AED), and Saudi Arabia (SAR). Simply enter your home price, down payment, annual interest rate, and loan term to instantly see your monthly payment.

For a more complete estimate, switch to “+ Tax & Insurance” to include annual property tax, home insurance, and PMI (private mortgage insurance) if your down payment is below 20%.

Mortgage Formula Used

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

  • P = Loan amount (home price − down payment)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Number of monthly instalments (years × 12)

Country-Specific Notes

  • Pakistan: Home finance is typically priced at KIBOR + bank spread. As of October 2026, the SBP policy rate is 11.5% and 1-year KIBOR is around 12.3%, with typical bank home-finance rates around 15.3%[reference:5]. The State Bank caps total loan repayments at 50% of net monthly income (Debt Burden Ratio)[reference:6].
  • India: Under the old tax regime, home loan interest is deductible up to ₹2,00,000 under Section 24(b), and principal repayment up to ₹1,50,000 under Section 80C. These deductions are not available under the new tax regime[reference:7].
  • United States: The full monthly payment is often called PITI — Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. If your down payment is below 20%, lenders typically require PMI, which is usually 0.3%–1.5% of the loan amount annually[reference:8].
