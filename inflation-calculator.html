How to Use This Inflation Calculator

This free inflation calculator works for any country and any currency. Select your country to load historical inflation data, enter an amount, and choose a start and end year to see how the value of money has changed.

For example, if you enter $1,000 in 2000 and select 2026 as the end year, the calculator shows what that $1,000 would be worth in 2026 based on the country's average inflation over that period. You can also enter a custom average annual inflation rate to model future scenarios.

Inflation Formula Used

The calculator uses the compound inflation formula:

FV = PV × (1 + r)n

  • FV = Future value (equivalent amount in the end year)
  • PV = Present value (the amount you enter)
  • r = Average annual inflation rate (as a decimal)
  • n = Number of years between start and end

When you leave the custom rate blank, the calculator uses the historical average annual inflation rate for the selected country over the chosen period, derived from official CPI data.

What Is Inflation?

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises, reducing purchasing power. When inflation occurs, each unit of currency buys fewer goods and services than before.

  • CPI (Consumer Price Index) is the most common measure of inflation. It tracks the price of a basket of goods and services over time.
  • Purchasing power is the value of money in terms of what it can buy. Inflation erodes purchasing power.
  • Deflation is the opposite of inflation — a general decline in prices. It is rarer but can occur during economic downturns.

Frequently Asked Questions (Global)

Is this inflation calculator free? Yes. All SitPanda calculators are 100% free with no signup required.

Does it work for any country? Yes. Select your country from the dropdown to load historical inflation data, or enter a custom rate. The calculator supports all major currencies and formats amounts according to your locale.

How accurate is the historical data? The calculator uses official CPI data from sources like the IMF, World Bank, and national statistics agencies. However, inflation baskets and methodologies vary by country, and historical revisions can occur. For precise research, always consult the original data source.

What is the difference between CPI and inflation rate? CPI is an index number that tracks the price level. The inflation rate is the percentage change in CPI over a period. The calculator uses the inflation rate derived from CPI changes.

Can I calculate future inflation? Yes. Enter a custom average annual inflation rate (e.g., 3%) and select a future end year. The calculator will project the future value based on that assumed rate. Remember that future inflation is uncertain — this is a projection, not a guarantee.

Why does the calculator ask for a custom rate? The custom rate lets you override the historical average with your own assumption. This is useful for modelling different scenarios, such as higher or lower future inflation, or for countries with limited historical data.

Does inflation affect all prices equally? No. Inflation is an average — some prices rise faster than others, and some may even fall. The CPI basket weights differ by country, so your personal experience of inflation may differ from the official rate.

What is hyperinflation? Hyperinflation is extremely high and accelerating inflation, often defined as exceeding 50% per month. It is rare but has occurred in several countries during economic crises.
