How to Use This Zakat Calculator

Enter your cash savings, gold and silver (in grams), investments, and business assets. Subtract your immediate debts. The calculator will automatically compare your net wealth to the Nisab threshold and show your Zakat due.

Zakat Formula Used

  • Net Wealth = (Cash + Gold Value + Silver Value + Investments + Business) − Debts
  • Nisab Threshold = Selected Nisab Weight × Current Price per gram
  • Zakat Due = If Net Wealth ≥ Nisab, then 2.5% of Net Wealth.
