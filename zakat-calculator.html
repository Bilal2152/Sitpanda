How to Use This Zakat Calculator

Zakat is one of the Five Pillars of Islam and an obligatory form of charity for Muslims who meet the minimum wealth threshold (Nisab). This calculator helps you determine the exact amount you need to pay annually.

Enter your cash savings, the weight of your gold and silver (in grams), the current value of your investments, and subtract any immediate debts you owe. The calculator will automatically determine if you are eligible to pay Zakat and how much.

Zakat Formula Used

  • Net Wealth = (Cash + Value of Gold + Value of Silver + Investments) − Debts
  • Nisab Threshold = 595 grams of Silver × Current Silver Price per gram
  • Zakat Due = If Net Wealth ≥ Nisab, then 2.5% of Net Wealth.

Gold vs Silver Nisab

There are two standards for Nisab in Islamic jurisprudence:

  • Gold Standard: 87.48 grams of gold.
  • Silver Standard: 612.36 grams of silver (often rounded to 595g or 612g depending on the scholar).

Most contemporary scholars recommend using the Silver Standard because its lower monetary value means more people qualify to pay Zakat, which benefits the poor. This calculator uses the silver standard (595g) by default. You can adjust the silver price in the settings to reflect your local market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Zakat calculator free? Yes, all SitPanda tools are 100% free with no signup required.

Do I pay Zakat on my home or car? No. Zakat is not payable on personal-use items like your primary residence, personal car, or furniture. It is payable on wealth that has the potential to grow (cash, gold, silver, business stock, investments).

What is the Zakat rate? The standard rate for Zakat on monetary wealth and trade goods is 2.5% (or 1/40th).

When is Zakat due? Zakat becomes due when your wealth exceeds the Nisab and has been in your possession for one full lunar year (Hawl).

How often do I need to update gold and silver prices? Gold and silver prices change daily. For the most accurate calculation, please check the current market price in your country and manually enter it into the calculator settings.
