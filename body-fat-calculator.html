✔ Verified Formula: This calculator uses the US Navy Method formula (based on the circumference of the neck, waist, and hips). While highly popular, tape measurements can have a margin of error. This tool is for educational purposes—consult a healthcare professional for clinical assessments.

How to Measure Correctly

  • Neck: Measure at the narrowest point, just below the Adam's apple.
  • Waist: Measure at the navel (for men) or at the narrowest point (for women). Do not pull the tape too tight.
  • Hips: Measure at the widest point of the buttocks (required for women).

Body Fat Categories (ACE Standards)

Men: Essential (2-5%), Athletes (6-13%), Fitness (14-17%), Average (18-24%), Obese (25%+).

Women: Essential (10-13%), Athletes (14-20%), Fitness (21-24%), Average (25-31%), Obese (32%+).
