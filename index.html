What each case option does

  • Sentence case capitalises the first letter of each sentence.
  • Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word.
  • camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case are naming styles used in code, URLs and file names.

Frequently asked questions

How does the word counter count words?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. This works for English, Urdu, Arabic and most languages that use spaces between words.

Does it count spaces as characters?

Both counts are shown: characters with spaces and characters without spaces. Use whichever your platform requires.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute and speaking time about 130 words per minute, which are common averages for adults.
