How to Use This Calculator

Enter your current age, desired retirement age, current savings, monthly contribution, expected return rate, and inflation rate. The calculator will project how much money you will have when you retire.

What is the 4% Rule?

The 4% rule is a widely used financial guideline suggesting that you can safely withdraw 4% of your retirement portfolio's value in the first year of retirement, and then adjust that amount for inflation each subsequent year. This gives you an estimate of your monthly income in retirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why account for inflation? Inflation reduces purchasing power. $1 million today will not buy the same amount of goods 30 years from now. We adjust the final number to show its value in today's money.

What return should I assume? For a diversified stock portfolio, a 7% average annual return is common. For a conservative portfolio, 4-5% may be more appropriate.
