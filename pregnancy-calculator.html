How is the Due Date Calculated?

Pregnancy lasts about 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last menstrual period. Doctors use Naegele's Rule, which adds 280 days to your LMP date. However, if you know your exact conception date (or IVF transfer date), we can calculate a more precise due date.

What are Trimesters?

  • 1st Trimester (Weeks 1-13): Baby's major organs begin to form.
  • 2nd Trimester (Weeks 14-27): Often called the "golden period" as morning sickness usually fades.
  • 3rd Trimester (Weeks 28-40): Final growth and preparation for birth.
