How to Use This Income Tax Calculator

Because tax brackets differ greatly between countries, this calculator allows you to enter your own progressive tax brackets. By default, it is set up with a simple 4-tier progressive system.

To use it, simply enter your Gross Income and any Deductions you are eligible for. Then, adjust the tax bracket limits and rates to match the tax laws of your country.

What is a Progressive Tax?

A progressive tax system means that as your income increases, the tax rate you pay on the next unit of currency also increases. This is why it's called "marginal" tax. You do not pay the highest rate on all your income, only on the portion that falls into that highest bracket.

What is Effective Tax Rate?

The Effective Tax Rate is the average rate at which your income is taxed. It is calculated by dividing your Total Tax Owed by your Gross Income. It is always lower than your highest marginal tax rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my income tax calculated?

The calculator uses a progressive tax system. Your income is divided into brackets. Each portion of your income is taxed at the rate of its respective bracket, not your highest rate on the total amount.
