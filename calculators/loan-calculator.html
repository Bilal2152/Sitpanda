How to Use This Loan Calculator

This free tool works for any type of loan: personal loans, auto loans, student loans, or business loans. Simply enter the loan amount, the annual interest rate, and the term (in years or months).

The calculator will instantly show your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI), which is the fixed amount you need to pay every month. It also shows the total interest you'll pay over the life of the loan.

Loan Payment Formula

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula:

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
  • M = Monthly Payment (EMI)
  • P = Principal Loan Amount
  • r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total Number of Months (Years × 12)

Note: For 0% interest loans, the calculator uses M = P ÷ n.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a loan monthly payment (EMI) calculated?

The standard formula is M = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of months.

Can I use this calculator for a 0% interest loan?

Yes. If you enter 0% as the interest rate (common in Islamic banking or special promotions), the calculator automatically switches to a simple division formula (Loan Amount ÷ Number of Months).

What is the difference between flat rate and reducing balance?

This calculator uses the reducing balance method (most common globally), where interest is calculated on the remaining principal. A flat rate calculates interest on the original amount for the entire term, making it more expensive.
