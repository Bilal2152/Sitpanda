How to Use This Salary Calculator

This tool helps you quickly convert your income between different time frames. Whether you know your hourly wage and want to see your annual salary, or you know your yearly package and want to know what it means per month, this calculator does it instantly.

By default, it assumes a standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week and 5 days per week. You can adjust these numbers if you work part-time or have a different schedule.

Salary Conversion Formulas

The calculator uses standard conversion math based on the inputs you provide:

  • Hourly to Annual: Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × 52 weeks
  • Daily to Annual: Daily Rate × Days per Week × 52 weeks
  • Weekly to Annual: Weekly Pay × 52 weeks
  • Bi-Weekly to Annual: Bi-Weekly Pay × 26
  • Monthly to Annual: Monthly Pay × 12

Why Use a Salary Calculator?

  • Job Offers: Compare a new job offer against your current salary.
  • Freelancing: If you charge an hourly rate, see what that equals in annual terms.
  • Budgeting: Understand your weekly or monthly cash flow.
