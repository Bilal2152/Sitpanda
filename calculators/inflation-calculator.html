What is Inflation?

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises, and subsequently, the purchasing power of currency falls. In simple terms, it means that your money buys less tomorrow than it does today.

For example, if you have $100 today and inflation is 5% per year, you will need $105 next year to buy the exact same basket of goods.

Inflation Formula

The calculator uses the standard compound inflation formula:

Future Value = Present Value × (1 + Inflation Rate)Years
  • Future Value = What your current amount will need to be in the future to maintain the same purchasing power.
  • Present Value = The amount you have today.
  • Inflation Rate = The assumed average annual inflation rate (as a decimal).
  • Years = Number of years into the future.

How to Protect Against Inflation

  • Invest: Keeping money in a savings account with low interest means it loses value over time. Investing in stocks, mutual funds, or real estate historically outpaces inflation.
  • Gold & Commodities: These are often used as a hedge against inflation.
  • Negotiate Salary: Ensure your income grows at least at the rate of inflation to maintain your standard of living.
